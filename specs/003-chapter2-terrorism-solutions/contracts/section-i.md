### I.A Military Operations Assessment (~250 words)

**Purpose**: Acknowledge kinetic successes while establishing need for complementary approaches.

**Must Include**:
- Recognition of military operations' role in dismantling territorial control
- Reference to Zarb-e-Azb, Radd-ul-Fasaad outcomes
- Transition argument: military alone insufficient for lasting peace
- Citation of post-operation metrics (attack reduction vs. continued low-level violence)

**Must NOT Include**:
- Criticism of military institution directly
- Operational details of military campaigns
- Intelligence capability assessments

### I.B NAP Review and Revision (~350 words)

**Purpose**: Analyze NAP implementation gaps and propose revision priorities.

**Must Include**:
- Reference to Chapter 1's NAP analysis (5-7 of 20 points implemented)
- Specific NAP points requiring attention:
  - Madrasah registration (30-40% registered)
  - Counter-narrative development (limited outcomes)
  - NACTA strengthening (coordination gaps)
- Recommendations for NAP revision framework
- Implementation accountability mechanisms

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 2):
1. NAP revision with implementation timelines and accountability
2. NACTA institutional strengthening with clear mandate

### I.C Non-Kinetic Strategic Shift (~300 words)

**Purpose**: Propose comprehensive strategy balancing security with prevention.

**Must Include**:
- "Whole-of-government" approach definition
- Civilian-led prevention pillar alongside security
- Inter-provincial coordination mechanisms
- Civil society integration framework

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 2):
1. Civilian-led CT coordination body with provincial representation
2. Institutionalized civil society consultation mechanism
