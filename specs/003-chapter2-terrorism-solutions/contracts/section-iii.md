### III.A Whole-of-Nation CVE Framework (~300 words)

**Purpose**: Establish comprehensive prevention architecture beyond security responses.

**Must Include**:
- CVE definition and scope (per research.md: non-kinetic approaches to prevent radicalization)
- Multi-stakeholder model: government, civil society, religious leaders, educators
- Prevention pillars: early intervention, community resilience, exit pathways
- International best practice references (UN CTED, OSCE frameworks)
- NACTA coordination role

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 2):
1. National CVE strategy with clear implementation roadmap
2. Community-based prevention networks with civil society leadership

**Sensitive Content Protocol**:
- Frame as prevention and support, not surveillance
- Emphasize voluntary engagement
- Reference human rights considerations

### III.B Deradicalization Programs (~250 words)

**Purpose**: Present evidence-based rehabilitation and reintegration approaches.

**Must Include**:
- Sabaoon Centre model analysis (reference Chapter 1)
- Program components: psychological support, vocational training, family engagement
- Scalability considerations and challenges
- Distinction: disengagement vs. deradicalization
- Post-program monitoring and support

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 2):
1. Scaled deradicalization program based on Sabaoon model
2. Reintegration support system with community involvement

**Sensitive Content Protocol**:
- Emphasize voluntary participation
- Frame as rehabilitation, not punishment
- Include family and community support elements
