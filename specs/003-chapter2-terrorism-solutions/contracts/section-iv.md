### IV.A Development Interventions (~350 words)

**Purpose**: Address socio-economic factors enabling recruitment and support.

**Must Include**:
- Relative deprivation framework (reference Chapter 1 Gurr analysis)
- Education access and quality improvements
- Youth employment generation programs
- Healthcare and social services in vulnerable areas
- Economic opportunity in conflict-affected regions
- Poverty-terrorism caveat: acknowledge complexity, avoid simplistic causation

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 3):
1. Targeted education investment in merged districts and Balochistan
2. Youth employment programs linked to CT-affected areas
3. Infrastructure development prioritizing underserved regions

**Geographic Focus**:
- Merged districts (former FATA)
- Balochistan
- Southern Punjab (specific districts identified in Chapter 1)

### IV.B FATA/Merged Districts Integration (~350 words)

**Purpose**: Accelerate civilian governance extension to merged districts.

**Must Include**:
- 25th Amendment context and implementation status
- Administrative capacity gaps (reference Chapter 1)
- Judicial extension and access to justice
- Police and civilian security force development
- Service delivery mechanisms
- Development fund utilization

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 2):
1. Accelerated judicial and administrative integration timeline
2. Enhanced development fund allocation with accountability mechanisms

**Sensitive Content Protocol**:
- Acknowledge local concerns and grievances
- Present integration as opportunity, not imposition
- Reference consultative processes

### IV.C Border Security Measures (~300 words)

**Purpose**: Address cross-border movement while acknowledging constraints.

**Must Include**:
- Terrain and geographic challenges
- Diplomatic considerations with Afghanistan
- Border management infrastructure
- Fencing and checkpoint status
- Technology and surveillance limitations
- Coordination mechanisms with Afghan authorities
- Realistic assessment of achievable goals

**Recommendations Required** (minimum 2):
1. Enhanced border management infrastructure and technology
2. Bilateral engagement framework for cross-border coordination

**Sensitive Content Protocol**:
- Present multiple perspectives on border issues
- Acknowledge diplomatic complexity
- Focus on management rather than hermetic sealing
- Avoid intelligence capability details
- No tactical security recommendations
